Copilot model pricing

What each model charges per million tokens, about 750,000 words. You pay separately for what you send, the input, and for what the model writes, the output. Prices are in AI credits, the unit your Copilot budget is counted in: 1 credit = $0.01.

New here? The basics

  1. Prices are per tokenA token is about ¾ of a word. One coding-agent step sends roughly 50,000 of them: open files, history and your prompt.
  2. Reading is cheap, writing is notOutput costs 3–8× more than input, but agents send far more than they write. See what a budget buys →
  3. Pricier isn’t always betterSome low-cost models score close to the best on coding benchmarks. Compare price with scores → or put models side by side →
