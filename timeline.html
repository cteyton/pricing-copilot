    How is this calculated?

    Date. The day the provider released the model (data/release-dates.csv), not the day it reached Copilot.

    Price. GitHub’s list price per million input and output tokens, blended by the mix: r × input + (1 − r) × output, with r = 90% by default. Default tier only: long-context rates and caching are left out. It is today’s price for every model: we don’t keep a price history, so a model whose price changed since launch is shown at its current price.

    Score. An Artificial Analysis index, picked with the Score switch: Coding (default) or Intelligence, the broader one, often published sooner for new models. Models without the selected index can’t pass the threshold: they are hollow on the Price view and listed under the chart on the Score view.

    Threshold. On the Price view, models scoring below it are faded. The sentence above the chart compares the cheapest model reaching it today with the first one that did.

    • Copilot drops older models, so the timeline only starts with the oldest model it still offers: the trend over a longer period isn’t visible here.
    • Scores measure the model, not Copilot’s harness around it, and say nothing about how many tokens a task takes.
